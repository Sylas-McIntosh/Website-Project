Behind the Sound

Oxford to Global Soundscapes

Formed in Oxford in 2010, Glass Animals are an English indie-pop band known for their atmospheric, genre-blending soundscapes, hypnotic rhythms, and vivid storytelling.


Frontman Dave Bayley writes, produces, and designs much of the group's visual aesthetic, turning personal memories, electronic textures, and hip-hop influences into multi-platinum records enjoyed across the globe.

Key Milestones

2010

Formation in Oxford

School friends come together to record bedroom demos, creating their signature tropical-electronic vibe.

2014

Debut Album: ZABA

Breakthrough release featuring the viral hit Gooey, earning them critical acclaim and international festival spots.

2021-2022

Historic Billboard Run

Heat Waves reaches #1 on the Billboard Hot 100, breaking records for the longest climb to the top spot in chart history.

2024

Cosmic Evolution

Release of I Love You So F***ing Much, launching a new era of space-inspired themes and arena tours.
