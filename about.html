About the Band

Oxford to Global Soundscapes

Glass Animals are an English indie rock band formed in Oxford in 2010. Led by singer, songwriter, and producer Dave Bayley, the group also features Drew MacFarlane, Edmund Irwin-Singer, and Joe Seaward.


Known for their psych-pop rhythms, narrative-driven lyrics, and energetic live shows, they burst into global stardom with hit singles like Gooey and Heat Waves.
